ONBOARDING GUIDE (FR-1.4)

Connect Your Freight Invoices in 2 Minutes

RateGuard AI audits carrier invoices automatically by parsing PDF attachments sent directly from your freight carriers. Follow these simple steps to configure automated email forwarding.

1. Your Inbound Audit Address

Every invoice forwarded to this address is instantly ingested, decrypted, and audited against your contracted rate matrices.

UNIQUE AUDIT INBOX acme-parts@in.rateguard.app

2. Dedicated Carrier Dispute CC Address

When sending carrier billing disputes, CC our tracking alias. Our system automatically links the carrier's reply and detects credit memos:

DISPUTE RECOVERY TRACKING disputes+acme-parts@in.rateguard.app

3. Setup Forwarding Rules

Microsoft Outlook 365 (Web or Desktop)

  1. Go to Settings (gear icon) > Mail > Rules.
  2. Click Add new rule and name it "Forward LTL Carrier Invoices to RateGuard".
  3. Under Add a condition, select Sender address includes and paste your top carrier domains:
    @abf.com, @xpo.com, @rrts.com, @odfl.com, @saia.com, @estes-express.com
  4. Under Add an action, select Forward to and enter {your-slug}@in.rateguard.app.
  5. Check Stop processing more rules and click Save.

Google Workspace / Gmail

  1. Go to Settings > Filters and Blocked Addresses > Create a new filter.
  2. In the From field, enter: (@abf.com OR @xpo.com OR @rrts.com OR @odfl.com).
  3. Click Create filter, check Forward it to:, and select your RateGuard inbound address.
  4. Click Create filter to activate.
