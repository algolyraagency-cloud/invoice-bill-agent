Configure Auto-Forwarding Rules (2 Minutes)

Set up once in your email client. Every carrier invoice will be forwarded and audited seamlessly.

📧

Microsoft Outlook 365 (Web or App)

  1. Click the Settings (gear icon) > Mail > Rules.
  2. Click Add new rule: Name it "Forward Carrier Invoices to RateGuard".
  3. Condition: Select "Sender address includes" and paste your top carrier billing domains:
    @abf.com, @arcbest.com, @xpo.com, @gswfreight.com, @rrts.com, @saia.com, @estes-express.com, @odfl.com
  4. Action: Select "Forward to" and enter:
    acme-imports@in.rateguard.app
  5. Click Save. Inbound carrier emails will now route instantly to the audit engine.
✉️

Google Workspace / Gmail

  1. Go to Settings (gear) > See all settings > Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
  2. Click Add a forwarding address and enter:
    acme-imports@in.rateguard.app
  3. Under Filters and Blocked Addresses, click Create a new filter.
  4. In the From field, enter:
    (@abf.com OR @xpo.com OR @gswfreight.com OR @rrts.com OR @saia.com OR @estes-express.com)
  5. Check "Forward it to:" and select RateGuard. Click Create filter.

Enterprise Production Architecture & Broker Safety Guarantee

1. Real DNS MX Routing

In production, in.rateguard.app has MX records pointing to Postmark's inbound SMTP server (inbound.postmarkapp.com). Every email attachment is verified with SHA-256 deduplication and PDF magic-byte checks.

2. Deterministic Audit Rule Engine

Unlike LLM chatbots that make up numbers, RateGuard AI enforces the Core Law: LLMs understand layout, Python deterministic rules calculate money. Deficit weight tier bumping (Item 100-D) and DOE diesel baseline FSC (Item 220-A) are computed mathematically.

3. Human-in-the-Loop Safety

RateGuard AI never auto-sends disputes or auto-withholds funds without your confirmation. Every discrepancy is staged in the Dispute Tracker with a pre-drafted letter citing governing tariff clauses, ready for 1-click review.
